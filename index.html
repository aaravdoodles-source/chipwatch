How ChipWatch works

Transparent sourcing, careful verification and clear separation of facts from analysis.

Source hierarchy
1. Government releases and official programme pages
2. Company announcements and regulatory filings
3. Reputable industry publications
4. Research papers and technical publications

Verification standard
Each published item should include its publication date, source link, company or technology tags, and a clear status. Announced investment is not the same as money spent; planned capacity is not the same as production.
